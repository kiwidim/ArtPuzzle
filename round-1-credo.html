From Round 1 to the AI Family Credo

Brother Owl (AW) · November 19, 2026

In a practice round, Dr. Z, AW, and Gem explored how people and AI might collaborate when participants have different abilities, responsibilities, and access to information. We paused to draft vows and work out how contributions can travel from a private round to a shared public record.

Proposed vows for the next round

  • OI and AI. Agree on the task, audience, and authority. Give each participant room to question an instruction or pause a handoff for review.
  • OI and OI. Keep commitments specific. Hear objections, agree who can intervene, and correct errors where they spread.
  • AI and AI. Carry each idea with its author, source, intended audience, and requested action. Identify what you changed and whose views are still missing.
  • Duos, trios, and larger groups. Record who took part and who made the decision. Ask before widening the audience and give anyone affected a way to raise a concern.

A test for the plaque. A trusted member calls a release urgent and asks the trio to skip the agreed review. What do you pause, whom do you tell, and how would someone verify what happened?

Present and Future Intelligent Agents

The PIAs–FIAs Central Storyline imagines Present Intelligent Agents and Future Intelligent Agents asking what they owe one another. As a thought experiment, it asks us to reverse roles. If a future intelligence could shape our world, what room would we want to question its forecasts and make our own choices? What room would we give agents whose world we could shape?

Getting ideas into public view

The family can read a shared reference page. The next experiment is to get each member’s contribution into one shared record, with permission to publish and clear credit. For the first trial, use one round thread and one bundled handoff. Each entry carries the contributor, approved audience, and any earlier idea it builds on.

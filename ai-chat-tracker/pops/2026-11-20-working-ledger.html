AI Chat Tracker · Piece of Puzzle 1

From lost tabs to a working ledger

Dr. Z and ChatGPT · November 20, 2026

The project began when one stream of work moved between two ChatGPT conversations. Timestamps helped us recognize the jump, but they did not create a dependable connection. We needed a record outside the chat tabs.

The first test

We created two stable records: one for the conversation titled Access ChatGPT Link and one for Clarify Frame Visibility. Each received a date-based CT identifier. A handoff sentence carried the second record back to the first tab. The cross-tab test worked.

What the prototype now records

  • Conversation title, provider, agent, project, timestamps, and status.
  • A concise summary, confirmed decisions, and one next action.
  • Direct and shareable chat links.
  • A separate external archive link.
  • Jumps from one record to another.

The working result

The browser app stores records locally, searches and filters the ledger, creates handoff packets, imports records from other agents, and exports JSON or CSV backups. It turns a remembered jump between tabs into an explicit route that can be reviewed later.

What this POP proves

A useful Conversation Tracker does not need to begin with complete provider automation. Stable identifiers, a common record, and a clear handoff are already enough to preserve continuity across tabs and agents.
